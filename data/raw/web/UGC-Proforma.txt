RV University - UGC Proforma Academic Policies

Assessment Framework:
- Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE): 70% weightage
- Semester End Examination (SEE): 30% weightage
- UG pass requirement: Above 40% in both CIE and SEE
- PG pass requirement: Above 50% in both CIE and SEE
- Students must maintain 80% attendance in every course

Progression Policy:
- Students with FI (Failed in Internals) or SA (Shortage of Attendance) may register in summer semester
- Maximum 12 credits or 4 courses (whichever is minimum) in summer semester
- A student must earn 80% of total credits in an academic year to be promoted to the next year

Fee Structure (set by Fee Regulatory Committee and Board of Governors):
- B.Tech (AIML, Data Science, Cloud Computing, Cybersecurity): CET seats as per GOK; JEE Mains ₹5,00,000/yr; MQ ₹10,00,000/yr
- B.Sc Economics: ₹1,20,000/yr + ₹16,000 skill/exam fees
- BBA: ₹2,25,000–₹2,50,000/yr + ₹16,000
- MBA: ₹5,50,000–₹6,25,000/yr + ₹16,000
