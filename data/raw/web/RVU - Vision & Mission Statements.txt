 
SCHOOL OF LIBERAL ARTS & SCIENCES 
Vision 
• To be a globally recognized, technology-driven School of Liberal Arts and Sciences 
that nurtures critical thinkers, creative problem-solvers, and compassionate leaders 
through interdisciplinary learning, ethical inquiry, and innovative education 
Mission 
• To pursue academic excellence through innovative pedagogy, interdisciplinary 
research, and experiential learning that integrates the Arts, Humanities, and Sciences 
and equip students with the skills necessary for diverse careers and lifelong learning 
• To prepare students to navigate an evolving world with adaptability and confidence 
by cultivating intellectual curiosity and embracing emerging technologies 
• Building a diverse academic community through national and international 
collaborations, fostering global perspectives while addressing local and regional 
challenges 
• Encouraging personal and professional growth by instilling empathy and civic 
responsibility whilst preparing students to become socially conscious and 
responsible human beings

SCHOOL OF ALLIED AND HEALTHCARE PROFESSIONS 
Vision 
• To be a world-class, technology-driven school dedicated to excellence in allied and 
healthcare education, research, and innovation, producing skilled, ethical, and 
compassionate professionals who contribute to global healthcare advancements and 
societal well-being.  
Mission 
• Strive for high standards in teaching, research, and capacity building in alignment 
with global benchmarks, fostering an evidence-based, interdisciplinary approach to 
allied and healthcare education. 
• Utilize emerging healthcare technologies, AI, and digital platforms to enhance 
education, clinical training, and research, ensuring inclusivity, accessibility, and 
future-readiness in healthcare professions. 
• Build a diverse, global academic community by fostering strong national and 
international collaborations with healthcare institutions, industry leaders, and 
research organizations to promote mobility, professional development, and 
institutional excellence. 
• Integrate theory with practical application through field-based learning, community 
outreach, and patient-centred care, ensuring professionals uphold the highest ethical 
and regulatory standards and contribute to sustainable healthcare systems and public 
health policies.
