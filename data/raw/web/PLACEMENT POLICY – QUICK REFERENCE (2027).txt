1. PLACEMENT GOVERNANCE 
● 
Career & Alumni Relations (CAR) coordinates all placement and internship activities across Schools. 
● 
CAR facilitates processes but does not adjudicate disciplinary matters. 
● 
All placement-related violations are formally referred to the Student Disciplinary Committee (STDC) under 
the University’s Student Code of Conduct. 
2. STUDENT ELIGIBILITY (SUMMARY) 
A student may participate in placements only if all the following are satisfied: 
● 
No academic backlogs at the time of registering for placement drives (unless permitted by a recruiter). 
● 
Minimum 80% attendance in mandatory pre-placement training. 
● 
Submission of the Placement Registration & Declaration Form. 
● 
Completion of required experiential components (internship / immersion / capstone), unless exceptionally 
approved by School leadership. 
● 
Compliance with all company-specific eligibility criteria. 
● 
No pending disciplinary case with STDC. 
3. PRE-PLACEMENT TRAINING (MANDATORY) 
Training consists of: 
1. Domain / Technical Training (School-specific) 
2. Soft Skills Training 
3. Emotional, Behaviour & Networking Intelligence Training 
Minimum 80% attendance across all components is compulsory. 
Any restriction due to non-compliance is decided by the Dean of the School, in consultation with CAR. 
4. OFFER RULES – KEY POINTS 
4.1 One Offer Rule (1×) 
● 
A student is considered placed (1×) once their name appears in a recruiter’s final selection list, irrespective of 
acceptance or rejection. 
4.2 Offer Progression Rule (1.5×) — ACTIVATION CONDITION 
The 1.5× opportunity is not automatic. 
It becomes applicable only when: 
● 
At least 40% of eligible students of the concerned School are placed, OR 
● 
The Dean of the School formally announces activation, whichever is earlier. 
Until activation: 
● 
Students are expected to apply carefully and responsibly. 
● 
Participation in higher-value opportunities is governed by institutional discretion and fairness. 
Once activated: 
● 
A student may attend up to four (4) additional placement drives, provided: 
o 
Existing offer CTC is below ₹8 LPA, and 
o 
The new offer is at least 1.5× the current offer. 
After exhausting this progression, eligibility shifts only to Dream Offer opportunities. 
 
 


 
4.3 Maximum Placement Opportunities 
● 
A student shall be provided a maximum of five notified (5) placement opportunities/attempts facilitated by 
RV University during a placement cycle. 
● 
At the Dean’s discretion, up to two additional opportunities may be provided once 40% of eligible students 
of the School are placed, in consultation with CAR. 
● 
Dream Company Opportunities, if declared by the Dean in consultation with CAR, may be exempted from this 
limit. 
5. DREAM OFFER – CONCEPT 
● 
A Dream Offer is an offer significantly higher than the historical highest CTC achieved by the School. 
● 
The benchmark is dynamic, progressive, and School-specific. 
Illustration: 
If a School’s historical highest CTC is ₹30 LPA and a later offer of ₹35 LPA is received, the Dream Offer benchmark 
thereafter stands revised to ₹35 LPA or higher for subsequent opportunities. 
6. STUDENT RESPONSIBILITIES 
● 
Apply only to roles and organisations the student is genuinely willing to join. 
● 
Maintain professional conduct at all stages. 
● 
Honour offers once selected, in line with institutional ethics and industry expectations. 
● 
Report any exceptional concerns promptly to CAR. 
Any action resulting in reputational, institutional, or peer-level impact may be referred to STDC. 
7. DISCIPLINARY HANDLING (SUMMARY) 
● 
CAR only forwards cases; it does not impose penalties. 
● 
All violations are examined by STDC. 
● 
Upon referral to STDC, the student shall not be permitted to participate in any placement-related activity 
until a final decision is issued. 
● 
Outcomes, penalties, reinstatement, and appeals follow STDC procedures.
