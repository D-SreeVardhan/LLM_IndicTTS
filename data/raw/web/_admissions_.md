## Admissions Process

Follow these simple steps to begin your journey at RV University:

###### Step 01

###### Register Yourself

Create your account on the RV University admissions portal using your basic details.

###### Step 02

###### Verify Your Email

Check your inbox and verify your email ID to activate your account.

###### Step 03

###### Start Your Application

Log in and begin filling in the application form with your academic details, personal information, and programme preferences.

###### Step 04

###### Pay the Application Fee

Proceed to pay the application fee online through the secure payment gateway.

###### Step 05

###### Complete and Submit

Review all the information entered, upload the required documents, and submit your application.

Once submitted, our admissions team will reach out with further steps regarding interviews or assessments, if applicable.

## Financial Aid & Support

At RV University, we believe that financial circumstances should never stand between a student and an exceptional education. Our financial aid framework is designed to recognise merit and ensure that the right support reaches those who need it — at every stage of your academic journey.

#### Scholarship for Children of RVEI Staff

RV University offers an annual scholarship of Rs. 25,000 to children of full-time employees of Rashtreeya Vidyalaya Educational Institutions (RVEI), including RV University and RVCE. This scholarship is renewable each year, subject to the continuation of the parent’s employment and the student’s satisfactory academic and disciplinary record. It reflects RVU’s commitment to supporting the families who are an integral part of its institutional fabric.

* RV University offers an annual scholarship of ₹25,000 to the children of employees working with Rashtreeya Vidyalaya Educational Institutions (RVEI), including RV University and RVCE.
* This scholarship is renewable each year, subject to the continuation of the parent’s employment and the student’s satisfactory academic and disciplinary record.
* It reflects RVU’s commitment to supporting the families who are an integral part of its institutional fabric.

#### Sporting and Special Category Scholarships

RVU recognises excellence beyond academics. Students who have represented their state or country in recognised sporting events are eligible for dedicated sports scholarships — up to 35% of tuition fee for national-level athletes and up to 50% for those competing at the international level. The RVU Alumni Scholarship additionally offers a tuition fee waiver of Rs. 50,000 for RVU graduates pursuing a master’s programme at the University.

* RVU recognises excellence beyond academics and offers Sporting and Special Category Scholarships to students with national or international level achievements.
* Eligible students include those who have represented the state or country in recognised sporting events or competitions.
* The quantum of scholarship is determined based on performance level, documentation, and committee review.
* These scholarships aim to encourage holistic growth and support students in balancing their academic and extracurricular pursuits.

## Cancellation and Refund Policy

For the AY 2026-27 as per UGC guidelines letter dated 15 August 2026, Ref. No. D.O.No.F.12-5/2024 (FNT/NET), the cancelation and refund dates of RV University are listed below.

#### UG & PG Programmes 2026-27

| **Date of Application for Cancellation** | **Percentage of Refund from First Year Full Fees** |
| --- | --- |
| Before 15 August 2026 | 100 % Refund |
| Between 16 August 2026 to 30 August 2026 | 90 % Refund |
| Between 31 August 2026 to 15 September 2026 | 80 % Refund |
| Between 16 September 2026 to 30 September 2026 | 50 % Refund |
| After 1 October 2026 | 0 % Refund |

* In case the admission of the student is cancelled on account of disciplinary action or violation of Anti Ragging Regulations or breach of code of conduct or any other Rules and Regulations of the University, no refund of fees is permissible.
* Entrance Examinations Fees / Application Form Fees are non – refundable.
* In any other case which is not covered in the above Rules, the decision would be taken by the Vice Chancellor.
* Under the circumstance of cancellation because of any disciplinary action no refund will be paid against any Academic and/ or Hostel and/ or Other Fees.
