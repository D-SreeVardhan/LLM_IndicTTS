# RVU Campus Guide (synthetic demo data)

This guide is synthetic demonstration content written for the course project. It describes typical campus
services so the assistant can answer everyday student questions. It is not official RV University information;
students should confirm details with the office named in each section.

## Campus location and how to reach

RV University is located at RV Vidyaniketan Post, 8th Mile, Mysuru Road, Bengaluru 560059, on the same
campus as the RV Educational Institutions. The campus is about 13 kilometres from Majestic. BMTC buses on
Mysuru Road stop at the RV College gate, and the university shuttle connects the campus with the nearest
Namma Metro station on the Purple Line every 20 minutes between 7:30 am and 9:30 am and between 4:00 pm
and 6:30 pm on working days. Visitors must register at the main gate security desk with a photo ID.

## Campus hours and student ID card

The campus is open from 7:00 am to 9:00 pm on working days and from 8:00 am to 6:00 pm on Saturdays.
Every student receives an RFID student ID card within two weeks of admission. The ID card must be worn
on campus and is needed to enter the campus, borrow library books, use the gym and take the university
bus. A lost ID card is reported to the Student Services office, and a duplicate card is issued within three
working days for a charge of 500 rupees.

## Hostel accommodation

RVU provides separate hostels for men and women inside the campus, with wardens living on site. Rooms
are available on twin sharing and triple sharing. The hostel fee for 2026-27 is 2 lakh 10 thousand rupees
per year for twin sharing and 1 lakh 80 thousand rupees per year for triple sharing, including four meals a
day, Wi-Fi, laundry twice a week and housekeeping. Students apply for a hostel room through the student
portal after paying the first instalment of their programme dues; rooms are allotted on a first come, first served
basis. Hostel students must be back in the hostel by 9:30 pm. Hostel queries go to hostel@rvu.edu.in.

## Transport and university buses

The university runs buses on 24 routes across Bengaluru, covering areas such as Jayanagar, Banashankari,
Rajajinagar, Vijayanagar, Electronic City, Whitefield, Yelahanka and Kengeri. Buses reach the campus by
8:45 am and leave the campus at 4:45 pm, with a late bus at 6:30 pm on Mondays, Wednesdays and Fridays.
The transport fee for 2026-27 is 48 thousand rupees per year, paid separately from the programme dues. Students
register for a route through the student portal before the semester starts and are given a bus pass on their
ID card. Route changes are handled by the Transport Office at transport@rvu.edu.in.

## Library

The central library is open from 8:00 am to 8:00 pm on working days and from 9:00 am to 5:00 pm on
Saturdays. During semester end examinations it stays open until 10:00 pm. Undergraduate students can
borrow up to four books for 14 days and postgraduate students up to six books for 21 days. Books can be
renewed twice online if no one has reserved them. The late return fine is 5 rupees per book per day. The
library provides e-journals and e-books from IEEE, Springer, ScienceDirect, JSTOR and EBSCO, which students
can access from home through the remote access login. Group study rooms are booked at the front desk.

## Food court and cafeteria

The campus food court is open from 7:30 am to 7:30 pm and has counters for South Indian, North Indian and
Chinese food, fresh juice, coffee and snacks. A full meal costs between 80 and 150 rupees. Payment is by
UPI, card or the prepaid wallet on the student ID card. A smaller cafe near the library is open from 8:00 am
to 8:00 pm. Drinking water stations with purified water are available on every floor of every building.

## Wi-Fi and IT helpdesk

The whole campus, including hostels, is covered by the RVU-Student Wi-Fi network. Students log in with their
university email ID and password, and up to three devices can be registered per student. Every student gets
a university Google Workspace account and access to licensed software such as Microsoft Office and
MATLAB. The IT helpdesk in the Admin Block is open from 9:00 am to 5:30 pm on working days, and problems
can also be reported to ithelpdesk@rvu.edu.in.

## Student portal and learning management system

Course registration, attendance, internal marks, payments, hostel and transport requests and certificate
requests are all handled through the RVU student portal. Lecture notes, assignments and quizzes are posted
on the learning management system, and students should check it every day. Login details for both are
sent to the student's university email after admission. A forgotten password is reset from the login page
or by the IT helpdesk.

## Medical centre and health insurance

The campus medical centre in the ground floor of the Admin Block is open from 9:00 am to 5:00 pm on working
days, with a doctor and a nurse on duty. Basic medicines and first aid are free. An ambulance is available on
campus at all times, and serious cases are taken to the partner hospital on Mysuru Road. All students are
covered by a group health insurance policy of 1 lakh rupees per year, which is included in the other charges paid each year.
In an emergency at night, hostel students should call the warden or the security control room.

## Counselling and student wellbeing

Two full-time counsellors offer free and confidential counselling for stress, anxiety, homesickness,
relationships and academic pressure. Appointments are booked through the student portal or by email to
wellbeing@rvu.edu.in, and walk-in hours are 2:00 pm to 4:00 pm on weekdays. The wellbeing centre also runs
workshops on time management and exam stress before every semester end examination.

## Anti-ragging

Ragging in any form is a criminal offence and RV University has zero tolerance for it. The university has an
Anti-Ragging Committee and an Anti-Ragging Squad that makes surprise checks in hostels, buses and the food
court. Students and parents must submit an online anti-ragging undertaking at the time of admission. Any
student who faces or sees ragging should report it to the Anti-Ragging Committee, the Dean of Student
Affairs or the national anti-ragging helpline 1800-180-5522. As the admissions policy states, a student whose
admission is cancelled for violating the anti-ragging regulations gets no refund.

## Grievance redressal and internal complaints

Students can raise academic or administrative grievances through the grievance section of the student
portal. Each grievance is acknowledged within two working days and resolved within 15 working days by the
Student Grievance Redressal Committee. Complaints of sexual harassment are handled confidentially by the
Internal Complaints Committee, which can be contacted at icc@rvu.edu.in. An Ombudsperson hears appeals
against decisions of the grievance committee.

## Clubs and student life

RVU has more than 40 student clubs, including coding, robotics, AI, entrepreneurship, debating, music, dance,
theatre, photography, film, quizzing and environment clubs, as well as NSS and NCC units. Students can join
clubs at the Club Fair held in the first month of the odd semester, or later through the club coordinators.
The annual cultural and technical festival is held in March, and every school also runs its own events and
hackathons. Club activities are scheduled after 3:30 pm so that they do not clash with classes.

## Sports facilities and gym

The campus has a football and cricket ground, basketball, volleyball and tennis courts, an indoor hall for
badminton, table tennis and chess, and a fitness centre. The gym is open from 6:00 am to 9:00 am and from
4:00 pm to 8:30 pm, with separate slots for women, and students enter with their ID card. Coaches are
available for the university teams in cricket, football, basketball, volleyball and athletics. Trials for
university teams are held in August. Students who play at state or national level can also apply for the
sports scholarship.

## Bonafide certificate, transcripts and other documents

Students request a bonafide certificate, study certificate, payment receipt copy, transcript or migration
certificate through the certificate section of the student portal. A bonafide certificate is issued within two
working days free of cost. A transcript is issued within seven working days at 1,000 rupees per
copy, and a migration certificate within ten working days. Documents are collected from the Registrar's
office in the Admin Block between 10:00 am and 4:00 pm, or sent by post at an extra charge.

## Attendance and leave

Students must maintain at least 80 percent attendance in every course. Attendance is marked by the faculty in
every class and can be checked on the student portal. A student who misses classes because of illness must
submit a leave application with a medical certificate to the Programme Head within three working days of
returning. Approved leave for representing the university in sports, NCC, NSS or cultural events is counted
as on duty. A student with a shortage of attendance gets an SA grade in that course and has to register for it
again, as described in the examination policy.

## Faculty mentors and academic advising

Every student is assigned a faculty mentor in the first week of the programme, and the same mentor usually
continues until graduation. Mentors meet their mentees at least once a month to review attendance, marks
and wellbeing, and they help with course choices, electives and internships. Students who are struggling in a
course can also attend remedial classes and peer tutoring sessions run by each school. Parents receive a
progress report after the internal assessments of every semester.

## Internships

Students do internships mainly during the semester break in June and July. The Career and Alumni Relations
office shares internship openings from partner companies, and students may also find internships on their
own. A student needs a no objection certificate from their school before starting an internship, and must
submit an internship report and a certificate from the company when they return. In many programmes the
internship carries credits, as given in the programme structure.

## International exchange and study abroad

The Office of International Relations offers semester exchange programmes with partner universities in
countries such as the United Kingdom, Germany, France, Australia and Singapore. Students usually apply in their
second or third year, and need a CGPA of at least 7.0 and no backlogs. Credits earned abroad are transferred
towards the RVU degree. Short summer schools abroad are also offered. Details are announced every
September, and the office can be reached at international@rvu.edu.in.

## Parking, dress code and conduct

Students who bring a two-wheeler or car must register it with the Security office and display the
university parking sticker; parking is in the student parking area near the main gate. There is no uniform,
but students are expected to dress decently, and formal dress is required for placement interviews and
presentations. Smoking, alcohol and drugs are strictly banned on campus. Students must follow the code of
conduct given in the student handbook.

## Orientation week for new students

New students attend a one-week orientation when they join. It covers a campus tour, an introduction to the
student portal and the learning management system, meetings with the Programme Head and the faculty mentor,
talks on anti-ragging, wellbeing and campus safety, and a Club Fair. Students collect their RFID ID card and
their university email login during this week. Parents are invited to a welcome session on the first day.

## Hostel rules, visitors and mess menu

Hostel residents sign the hostel register when they go out after 6:00 pm and must be back by 9:30 pm. A night
out or a weekend home visit needs a leave request approved by the warden and a message from a parent. Parents
may visit in the visitors' lounge between 10:00 am and 6:00 pm; other visitors are not allowed inside the rooms.
The mess serves breakfast from 7:00 am to 9:00 am, lunch from 12:30 pm to 2:00 pm, snacks at 5:00 pm and
dinner from 7:30 pm to 9:00 pm. The weekly menu has both South Indian and North Indian dishes, with a
vegetarian and a non-vegetarian counter, and is decided by a mess committee that includes student members.
Cooking appliances, alcohol and smoking are not allowed in hostel rooms.

## Laboratories, maker space and computing labs

Each school has teaching laboratories for its programmes, and the computing labs are open to all students
from 8:30 am to 7:00 pm on working days. The maker space in the Innovation Block has 3D printers, a laser
cutter, electronics workbenches and tools; students may use it after a short safety session and by booking a
slot on the student portal. High-performance GPU servers are available for final-year and research projects
on request through the faculty guide. Students must follow the lab safety rules and may not take equipment out
of a lab without permission.

## Innovation, incubation and startups

The RVU Innovation and Incubation Centre supports students who want to build a startup. It runs an ideation
bootcamp every semester, gives selected teams free co-working space, mentoring from industry experts and
seed grants of up to 5 lakh rupees, and helps them file patents. Students can apply with a short idea pitch
through the centre's page on the student portal. The Entrepreneurship Club organises pitch days where teams
present to investors and alumni founders. Queries go to incubation@rvu.edu.in.

## Undergraduate research

Undergraduate students can work on research with faculty from their first year. Students approach a faculty
member whose area interests them, or apply to the research projects announced on the portal each semester.
The university gives small research grants of up to 50 thousand rupees for student projects and supports
students presenting papers at recognised conferences by paying the registration charge. Good projects can
be published with the faculty member as co-author. The Office of Research is in the Admin Block and can be
reached at research@rvu.edu.in.

## Online courses, MOOCs and value-added courses

Students may earn credits through approved online courses on SWAYAM and NPTEL; the credits count towards
the degree as open electives, up to the limit set by each school. Each school also offers short value-added
courses in areas such as data analytics, cloud computing, design thinking, foreign languages and public
speaking, which give a certificate but no credits. Students register for value-added courses on the student
portal at the start of each semester. Free access to Coursera courses is available through the university
licence for students who sign in with their university email.

## Language and communication support

The Centre for Languages offers free spoken English and professional communication classes for students who
want to improve their English, and basic Kannada classes for students from other states. Classes are held in
small groups after 3:30 pm. Foreign language courses in French, German, Japanese and Spanish are offered
as value-added courses. The writing centre helps students with reports, statements of purpose and resumes
by appointment.

## Students with disabilities and accessibility

All academic buildings have ramps, lifts and accessible washrooms, and wheelchairs are available at the main
gate security desk. Students with a disability should register with the Equal Opportunity Cell, which arranges
support such as a scribe, extra time in assessments, reserved seating in classrooms and accessible hostel
rooms. Screen-reader software is installed on selected library computers. The cell can be reached at
equalopportunity@rvu.edu.in.

## Campus safety and security

The campus is protected by security staff 24 hours a day and by CCTV cameras at the gates, in the buses,
hostels and common areas. Only people with a valid ID card or a visitor pass are allowed in. Women students
who stay late on campus can ask the security control room for an escort to the hostel or the bus stop. The
RVU Safe app on the phone has an emergency button that alerts the security control room with the student's
location. Students should report any suspicious person or activity to the nearest security guard.

## Emergency numbers

In an emergency on campus, students should call the security control room, which is open 24 hours a day and
can send the ambulance, the warden or the duty officer. National emergency numbers also work on campus:
112 for any emergency, 108 for an ambulance, 100 for the police and 101 for the fire service. Women in distress
can call the women's helpline 181. The national anti-ragging helpline is 1800-180-5522.

## Lost and found

Items found on campus are handed over to the security desk at the main gate, which keeps them for 30 days.
Students who lose something should check with the security desk and report it on the lost and found section
of the student portal with a description of the item. Lost ID cards should also be reported to Student
Services so that the card can be blocked. Items left in a university bus are kept at the Transport Office.

## Bank, ATM, stationery and printing

A branch of a nationalised bank with an ATM is located near the food court and is open from 10:00 am to 4:00 pm
on working days. The stationery shop next to the library sells notebooks, lab records and drawing materials
and also does printing, photocopying and binding. Printing costs 2 rupees per black and white page and
10 rupees per colour page, paid by UPI or the ID card wallet. Self-service printers in the library print from
the student's university email.

## Auditoriums, seminar halls and event booking

The campus has a main auditorium with 800 seats, an open-air amphitheatre and several seminar halls with 100
to 200 seats. Clubs and student groups book these venues through the events section of the student portal at
least seven days before the event, with approval from their faculty coordinator. Sound and projection
support is provided by the IT and events team. Events must end by 9:00 pm.

## Student council and class representatives

Each class elects two class representatives, one of them a woman, at the start of every academic year. The
Student Council is formed from representatives of each school and is led by a president and secretary who
are elected by students. The council takes student concerns to the university leadership, helps organise the
annual festival and sits on committees such as the mess committee and the grievance committee.

## Course feedback

At the middle and end of every semester, students give anonymous feedback on each course and its teacher
through the student portal. The feedback is reviewed by the Programme Head and the school's academic
committee and is used to improve teaching. Students can also raise concerns about a course with their class
representative or faculty mentor at any time.

## Laptops and software for students

A personal laptop is strongly recommended from the first year, and it is required in design, computer science
and data science programmes. A laptop with at least 8 GB of memory and a recent processor is enough for most
programmes. Licensed software such as Microsoft Office, MATLAB and Adobe Creative Cloud is provided free
through the university account. Students who cannot afford a laptop can borrow one from the library for up to
seven days.

## Academic integrity and plagiarism

All assignments, reports and theses are checked with plagiarism detection software. Copying another
person's work, buying assignments or submitting AI-generated text as one's own without permission is
treated as academic misconduct. A first case usually leads to zero marks for that piece of work, and repeated
cases are sent to the disciplinary committee. Students should cite every source they use, and the library runs
workshops on referencing and on using AI tools responsibly.

## NSS, NCC and volunteering

The National Service Scheme (NSS) unit runs village visits, blood donation camps, cleanliness drives and
teaching programmes for nearby government schools. The National Cadet Corps (NCC) unit trains students in
drill and leadership and prepares them for the B and C certificates. Students join NSS or NCC at the start of
the first year. Volunteering hours can count towards the community engagement credits in many programmes.

## Yoga, meditation and quiet spaces

Yes, there are free yoga classes on campus. Yoga classes are held every morning from 6:30 am to 7:30 am in
the indoor sports hall and are open to all students and staff without registration. Meditation classes are
held on Wednesday evenings from 5:00 pm to 6:00 pm. A quiet room for reflection and prayer is available on the ground floor of the
library, open to students of all faiths. International Yoga Day on 21 June is celebrated with a campus-wide session.

## Green campus and sustainability

The campus uses solar panels on the rooftops, rainwater harvesting and a sewage treatment plant that supplies
water for the gardens. Single-use plastic is banned on campus, and waste is sorted into wet, dry and
recyclable bins. The Environment Club runs tree planting drives and a cycle-sharing scheme with bicycles
available at the main gate for moving around the campus.

## Changes to name and personal details

A student who needs to correct their name, date of birth, phone number, address or parent details in the
university records submits a request through the student portal with supporting documents, such as the
Class 10 marks card for date of birth or a gazette notification for a change of name. Phone number and
address changes are updated within two working days; name and date of birth corrections are approved by the
Registrar and take up to 15 working days.

## Parents and communication

Parents receive an SMS and an email when their ward is absent for more than three days in a row, and they get
a progress report after every round of internal assessments. Parent-teacher meetings are held once a semester,
in person or online. Parents can raise concerns with the faculty mentor or with Student Services at
studentservices@rvu.edu.in, and they get a separate parent login to the student portal.

## Important contacts and office hours

Most offices are open from 9:00 am to 5:00 pm, Monday to Friday, and from 9:00 am to 1:00 pm on the second
and fourth Saturdays. Admissions questions go to admissions@rvu.edu.in, payment questions to the Accounts Office
at accounts@rvu.edu.in, examination questions to the Controller of Examinations at coe@rvu.edu.in, and
placement questions to Career and Alumni Relations at car@rvu.edu.in. General student questions go to
Student Services at studentservices@rvu.edu.in. The security control room on campus is open 24 hours a day.
